# App Scout privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

App Scout is a plugin that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what it does with data.

## What App Scout collects about you

Nothing. App Scout has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting. The author receives no data from your use of the plugin.

## What App Scout reads from the internet

When you run a scout, App Scout reads public pages and public APIs of the Apple App Store, Google Play, Chrome Web Store, Google Workspace Marketplace and GitHub. Those requests contain the search topic or category you asked about, and the usual information any web request carries (such as your IP address), which those services handle under their own privacy policies. App Scout sends no other data to them and no data to anyone else.

## What App Scout stores

Your research sessions (the apps collected, Claude's grouping, your questions and Claude's answers), your preferences and your exports are saved as files on your computer, in the plugin's data directory. They never leave your computer unless you share them. Uninstalling the plugin deletes that directory unless you choose to keep it.

## The local web server

App Scout runs a small web server reachable only from your own computer (127.0.0.1) so the map can open in your browser. Every request needs a random token created when the plugin starts.
